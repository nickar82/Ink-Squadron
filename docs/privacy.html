Ink Squadron – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Ink Squadron ("the game") is made by Nickar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address or contacts.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings – levels, stars, scores, achievements, language, sound settings and the pilot names you type. These stay on your phone and are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data. We never receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows full-screen ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (views and taps) and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Local multiplayer (Wi-Fi / Bluetooth)

The 1 VS 1 mode on two phones uses Google Nearby Connections. To find the other phone nearby, Android asks for Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi devices and (on older Android versions) location permission. This is used only to connect the two phones directly; game moves are exchanged between the two devices and are not sent to us. Location is not stored or collected.

Opening Google Play

If you tap "Rate the game", the Google Play Store opens. Anything you do there is covered by Google's own policies.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone, apart from the advertising data processed by Google AdMob described above.

Security and retention

We do not run our own servers for the game, so we hold no player data. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
